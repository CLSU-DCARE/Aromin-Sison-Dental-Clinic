Services

Care for every stage
of your smile.

From routine cleanings to full orthodontic plans, every treatment follows the same approach: a clear explanation, an honest recommendation, and the time it takes to get it right.

General & Preventive

Keeping every
smile healthy.

Cleaning & Check-Ups

Routine cleanings, oral exams, and early screening so small concerns never become big ones.

Fillings & Restorations

Tooth-colored fillings that repair decay or damage while keeping your smile looking natural.

Tooth Extraction

Gentle removal of damaged or impacted teeth, with a clear plan for what comes next.

Orthodontics

Straighter smiles,
built to last.

Braces & Aligners

Full orthodontic treatment plans with monthly adjustments and a clear payment schedule.

Retainers & Follow-Up

Post-treatment retainers and check-ins to protect the results once your braces come off.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Confidence you
can see.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening for a brighter smile, safely done under a dentist's supervision.

Veneers

Custom porcelain veneers to reshape, brighten, or repair the look of your front teeth.

Smile Design

A full treatment plan built around how you want your smile to look and feel, start to finish.

Restorative & Surgical

Repair, rebuild,
and restore.

Root Canal Therapy

Treatment that saves an infected or badly decayed tooth instead of removing it.

Crowns & Bridges

Durable caps and bridges that restore the strength and shape of damaged or missing teeth.

Oral Surgery

From wisdom teeth to more involved procedures, handled with a clear plan for recovery.

Diagnostics & Technology

guessing less.

Low-radiation digital imaging for faster, more precise diagnostics than traditional film.

Guided Imaging

Detailed 3D views used to plan orthodontic and restorative treatment with more accuracy.
