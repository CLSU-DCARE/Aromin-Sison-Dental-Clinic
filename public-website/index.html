Chapter One: Why We Started

A smile is a small thing that carries a lot: confidence, comfort, and the courage to be seen.

Aromin-Sison Dental Clinic began with a simple observation: great dentistry in the Philippines often felt clinical, rushed, or intimidating. We wanted to build something different: a clinic where the science is world-class, but the experience feels like being looked after by family.

Every treatment plan starts with a conversation, not a checklist. We take the time to explain what we see, what we recommend, and why: so you leave every visit understanding your own smile a little better.

Chapter Six: Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

Do I need an appointment?
We recommend booking ahead so we can match you with the right dentist and time slot, but we understand things come up: see our walk-in policy below.
What services do you offer?
General and cosmetic dentistry, cleanings and check-ups, orthodontics (braces), digital scanning, teeth whitening, and oral surgery. Ask us about a treatment plan tailored to your smile.
What are your clinic hours?
We're open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays and major holidays: any announcements about holiday closures are posted on our homepage.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, we welcome walk-ins whenever we have an open chair, though patients with a booked appointment are always seen first. Booking ahead means less waiting for you.
How do I book an appointment?
Use the booking form above, or log in to your patient account to choose a service, dentist, and time slot. If your preferred slot isn't available, you'll be guided to pick another date and time.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept cash, major debit and credit cards, and GCash. Braces and other extended treatment plans can be arranged with a monthly payment schedule.
