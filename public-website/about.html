About Aromin-Sison Dental Clinic

Four decades of care, built one patient at a time.

Since 1985, we've treated every visit as a chance to earn trust: not just fill a schedule. Here's the story of how that started, and the people who carry it forward today.

Our History

From a single chair to a full studio.

Aromin-Sison Dental Clinic opened its doors in 1985 in Muñoz, Nueva Ecija, founded on a simple belief: that great dental care shouldn't feel cold or rushed. What began as a single treatment room has grown, over four decades, into a full studio equipped with modern digital scanning, guided imaging, and a team trained in both general and cosmetic dentistry.

Through the years, the clinic has stayed in the hands of the same family, with Dr. Arsenia Aromin and Dr. Kathrine Sison carrying forward the founding philosophy while bringing in new techniques, technology, and a second generation of clinical staff. Thousands of patients across Central Luzon have grown up in our chair: some are now bringing in their own children.

What hasn't changed is the pace of every appointment: unhurried, explained clearly, and centered on the person in the chair rather than the clock on the wall.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision, and the promise
behind every visit.

Our Mission

To deliver honest, world-class dental care in an environment that feels warm rather than clinical: treating every patient the way we'd want our own family treated.

Our Vision

To be Central Luzon's most trusted dental studio: known as much for how patients are treated as for the quality of the work itself.

Our Promise

No surprises at checkout, no rushed explanations, and no procedure recommended that we wouldn't recommend to someone we love.
